This Session

Five fixes this session — cozier glass, no greeting, a phone-friendly modal, a safer swipe, and a stats bar that never scrolls away.

    top: auto; left: 0; right: 0; bottom: 0;
    transform: translateY(100%);

Key Learnings

A stale service worker runs old code. It served a pre-guard app.js next to a new index.html — one throw in init blanked the whole page. Bumping the cache to dojo-v5 + network-first keeps it coherent.

Glass-inside-glass reads heavy. The home focus card was a glass card holding glass mission cards. One tinted layer is enough — the outer wrapper came off.

Mobile 100vh lies. It's taller than the visible area, so a "92vh" sheet lost its top gap behind the address bar. dvh gives a real offset.

A sticky bar needs a background. Without the glass fill, mission cards scrolled straight under it and you read two things at once. Background + blur fixed it.
